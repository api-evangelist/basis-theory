Merchants often find themselves in the position of wanting, or needing to migrate to a new payment processor . A PSP migration is normally either to take advantage of lower fees or superior services, and rarely without challenges. A reliable way to migrate to a new payment processor without disrupting business is to store card data in an independent vault rather than with the processor itself.
